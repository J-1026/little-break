You vs your agent · How to play

  1. When the ball lands in a red cup, it's your turn. The number beside the cup is your key.
  2. Hold that key and the power bar starts moving. Let go to shoot.
  3. Stop in the green part to score. The white middle is a swish.
  4. Each cup is worth its number: cup 1 is closest to the hoop and easiest, cup 5 is farthest and hardest.
  5. A swish scores double, so a swish from cup 5 is worth 10.
  6. Shoot before the white ring around the cup runs out, or the ball pops out by itself.
  7. If the ball lands in a blue cup, it's the agent's turn. Miss into a blue cup and you've been stolen from.
  8. First to 21 wins.
  9. Switch views on the right. Pinch (or scroll) to zoom, drag an empty spot to turn the table.
